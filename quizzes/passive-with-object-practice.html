人は人に〜を〜られる

20 sentences practising the passive where the person affected is the topic, the doer takes に, and the thing affected keeps を. Type your attempt, then reveal the answer.

Pattern: A は B に (thing) を V-passive. Often the thing is something that belongs to A (a bag, a diary, a foot), and A is affected by what B did. A few sentences here are neutral or positive rather than unpleasant (being praised, being asked).
Conjugation: u-verbs: u→a + れる (買う→買われる). ru-verbs: drop る + られる (見る→見られる). する→される.
