Note on volitional: the volitional form (〜よう / 〜おう) has no past or negative of its own. For past and negative practice I've used common set phrases built on it: 〜(よ)うと思っていた (was thinking of doing) and 〜(よ)うとした / 〜(よ)うとしなかった (tried to / didn't try to).
